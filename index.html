A few useful details

Before you jump in.

Clear expectations from the start.

Can I install Zentry today?

Zentry is in an owner-tested private beta. Public installation, customer accounts, and billing are being prepared. Join the waitlist for launch updates.

What does the $10 plan include?

The planned price is $10 per month per user for full access to the individual CRM features shown here. Google service quotas and sending limits still apply. Team functionality is coming soon.

Does Zentry read my inbox or track opens?

No. The current beta does not scan your inbox or use open-tracking pixels. Mark replies manually so the sequence knows to stop following up.

Where does my CRM data live?

The beta uses Google Sheets for contacts and sending records, plus Apps Script properties for configuration. See our privacy notice for details about the beta and website providers.

How do recipients opt out?

Outreach includes an unsubscribe link. Confirming an opt-out stops that sender’s outreach in that spreadsheet. It is not a global opt-out from all senders or spreadsheets.
